# Style target — what "a good popular piano-solo sheet" looks like

Look at page 1 of two or three published sheets in the same genre before engraving a pop/film
piece — whatever the caller has, or what a publisher's preview shows. The three below are the
anchors this style was derived from; the observations hold whether or not you have the files.
- `Reflection - From Disney's Mulan - Sheet music transcription.pdf` (TranscriptionsByPaul): title,
  "From …", "As performed by …", "Music by …" block; tempo word **Moderately**; chord symbols above
  every harmonic change (F, Dm7, C/B♭, B♭m); grand staff; 4 bars per system, 4 systems per page;
  *with pedal* once under bar 1; LH mostly broken chords or held dyads; RH melody with chord tones;
  clef changes in the LH when it climbs; almost no dynamics.
- `Remember Me - From Disney Pixar Coco …pdf`: same house; **8va** lines over high RH passages
  instead of ledger lines; triplet bracket only where the music is triplet; accents on a few chords.
- `[Nice Piano Sheets] ロクデナシ - ただ声一つ.pdf` (a pop-cover channel's own house style): ♩ = 130 mark,
  no chord symbols, **8-------** (8va) lines, 3 bars per system, arpeggio LH written as plain eighths,
  melody in octaves when the original has them, key signature changes at modulations.
- `2628.菲道爾-能遇見就很不錯了 鋼琴原調獨奏樂譜 CM.pdf` (Chinese pop): chord symbols, **lyrics under
  the RH** for the sung melody, "Pn." staff label, 2–3 bars per system when lyrics are present.

Common denominator (= the rubric's "2" column):
1. Title block: title, subtitle (film/artist/"as performed by"), composer, arranger.
2. Tempo word and/or ♩ = N at bar 1; nothing else in the tempo line.
3. Chord symbols on pop/jazz/film pieces, one per harmonic change, slash bass when the bass differs.
4. Two staves, treble/bass, clef changes only for whole phrases; 8va rather than ≥ 3 ledger lines.
5. Melody on top with stems up when a second voice exists; accompaniment simple: broken chords as
   beamed eighths/sixteenths, block chords as one chord symbol, bass note held with a tie or a dot.
6. Ornaments as symbols or grace notes; runs as beamed groups, never confetti rests.
7. 3–5 bars per system, 4–5 systems per page; bar numbers at system starts; page numbers.
8. Very few dynamics (a *mp* at the start, a *cresc.* into the chorus); *with pedal* once.

Anti-patterns (from the v1 loop and from raw MuseScore MIDI imports):
- naturals on every note; 32nd/64th walls for ornaments; a rest after every melody note;
- LH chords wider than a 10th; a melody note buried in the LH staff; one bar per system;
- "Piano" printed at every system; two independent staves instead of one grand staff;
- tempo mark without a word, or eight dynamics per bar; pedal marks every beat.

Use the anchors for *style* only. Notes, rhythm and structure come from the MIDI and the piece's own
reference (dossier sheets or ground truth), never from an anchor of a different piece.
